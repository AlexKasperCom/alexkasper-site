Reader letters describe water-meter tampering methods and, in reply, the utility-side detection techniques (like computer-flagged consumption drops) that make them risky, with columnist Jim Phelps using the exchange as a springboard for broader commentary on energy waste, sewage-to-methane conversion, and solar water heating — including skepticism toward a utility-sponsored solar program's pricing.
