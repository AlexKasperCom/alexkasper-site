Recaps the July 29 NYC Phone Phreak Convention (attended openly by phone company agents) and restates YIPL's mission for new readers. Reports that John 'Captain Crunch' Draper, of Esquire magazine fame, faces wire fraud charges intended to intimidate phreaks broadly, and solicits donations to his defense fund alongside lawyer Jim McMillan. Publishes full schematics and construction notes for the Red Box (a 2.2kHz coin-tone oscillator) and passes along an unconfirmed rumor that the phone company is testing red-box detection in New York.
