Rosewood Key's own homepage describes the company as "Digital Publishing" and lists "Our brands" as Naturesign ("Digital regulatory, simplified. Instant regulatory documents for the flavor & fragrance industry") and Alex Kasper ("Deception Research. Cybersecurity educator and social engineering expert, co-creator of the world's first anti-social engineering certification"), confirming Rosewood Key, LLC as the parent entity behind both. It lists a shared contact address, phone number, and email with Naturesign: 1900 N Bayshore Drive Ste 1A-317, Miami, FL 33132-3002.
