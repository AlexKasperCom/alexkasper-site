'Lavoisier' provides an exhaustive reverse-engineered explanation of 'Milo Fonebill's' automatic blue box, a 10-digit-memory tone-dialing circuit, comparing its low standby current draw favorably against 'Peter Piper's' programmable box from issue #56. The article walks through the shift-register-based number entry, playback ('RUN'), and clearing sequences in detail, including exact clock timings and gate-level logic, framed as a corrected and annotated companion to Fonebill's original hand-drawn schematic.
