Publishes full build instructions for the 'Super-Duper Project' — an early black box circuit (switch, resistor, capacitor) that lets a home phone receive long-distance calls at no charge to the caller — with detailed timing instructions for answering and switching. Renews the call to boycott ITT, listing its subsidiaries (Sheraton Hotels, Wonder Bread, Twinkies) as war-profiteering targets, and shares a trick for placing stamps to dodge postmark cancellation. Reader letters continue prank-calling Southern Bell's chief of security.
