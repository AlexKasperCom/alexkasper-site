Reports Bell's phasing-out of glass phone booths for vandalism-resistant vinyl-and-metal designs ahead of a shift to single-slot coin phones by 1976, and plugs RESIST's 'Movement Security Kit' for handling police and grand jury encounters. Includes tricks for defeating sandwich vending machines and warnings about key theft from vending-machine restockers, a satirical item about a streaking championship, and continued calls for reader-contributed technical information across locks, radio, and alarms.
