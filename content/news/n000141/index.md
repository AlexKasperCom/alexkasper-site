YIPL formally renames itself the Technological American Party (TAP), explaining that the newsletter has outgrown pure phone phreaking to cover meters, locks, and chemistry, and asks readers to become primary contributors of new information. Appeals for donations to Abbie Hoffman's legal defense after his cocaine arrest, crediting him as instrumental to the newsletter's existence. Recaps the second annual Phone Phreak Convention, where phone company and DA agents openly monitored the Blue Box workshop and attendees wore masks to avoid being photographed.
