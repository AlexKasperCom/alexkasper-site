Marks TAP's fifth anniversary and reports John 'Captain Crunch' Draper's second arrest in San Francisco on wire fraud charges tied to blue-box calls, resulting in probation and bail. Tom Edison troubleshoots a common Red Box failure mode readers were reporting and solicits more off-hook current readings from around the country. Edison and a co-writer ('Jim') push back forcefully against readers who complained TAP was too political, defending its social commentary on pollution, nuclear power, and surveillance as inseparable from its technical mission.
