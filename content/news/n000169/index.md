Two collaborators nicknamed 'CJ' and 'The Professor' dissect a decommissioned 'Fortress' payphone, correcting and refining mechanical details from an earlier issue's coin-box article and explaining how the coin-relay mechanism actually generates its tone pulses. Also warns that Red Box tone timing drifts with outdoor temperature and walks through a series of coin-drop 'click' experiments readers could perform on live payphones to understand the mechanism, without needing to remove anything.
