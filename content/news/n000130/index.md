Promotes local 'Peoples Yellow Pages' directories in Boston and New York and urges readers to start their own chapters, framing every reader as an unofficial YIPL representative authorized to pitch local papers and radio stations on phone-phreaking features. Clarifies confusion over the 'Super-Duper Project' black box circuit versus the similar one printed in Abbie Hoffman's Steal This Book, explaining why YIPL's version works better. Includes toll-free loop numbers and pay-phone refund scripts from readers.
