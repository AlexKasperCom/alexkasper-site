Wikidata item Q138340698 is Alex Kasper's own biographical entry: birth name Alexis C. Kasperavicius, born April 14, 1968, in Cleveland; languages spoken include English, German, French, and Lithuanian; residences listed as Los Angeles (from 1971), Berlin (June 2013 to April 2022), and Montreal (from April 2022).

It records his role as co-founder of Nexspace (1999–2006), his participation in *Freedom Downtime* and *Takedown*, his status as plaintiff in *Kasper v. Pacific Bell Telephone Company* (linked to the Anthony Pellicano wiretapping scandal), and his musical career: horn as instrument, principal horn with the Sinfonie Orchester Schöneberg (2014–2022) and, from 2024, with the Orchestre symphonique de l'Isle.
