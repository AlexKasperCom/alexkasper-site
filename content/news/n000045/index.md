Jon D. Markman reports that Lewis De Payne surrendered in federal court in Los Angeles and was released on bond after being indicted alongside Kevin Mitnick. The article describes De Payne’s employment and background and outlines the specific conduct alleged in the indictment.

It also records the defense position that De Payne was targeted because of his longstanding friendship with Mitnick and includes discussion of the legal representation and strategy surrounding the case.
