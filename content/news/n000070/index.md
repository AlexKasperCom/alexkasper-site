This archived press-information page presents background material about Defensive Thinking, the security consultancy founded by Kevin Mitnick and Alex Kasper.

The page describes the company's focus on social-engineering awareness, security education, and methods for reducing human vulnerabilities within organizations. It also provides information about the Certified Social Engineering Prevention Specialist program, a training and certification initiative designed to teach participants how to recognize and defend against manipulation-based attacks.
