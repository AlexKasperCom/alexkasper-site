HOW IT WORKSWhen you call someone long distance, you are billed from the moment they |answer. The phone company knows you answer when a certain amount of electriccurrent flows through the phone. Inside the phone, connected across the F and RR terminals, isa capacitor, a device which allows more volume for your voice without usingany more electric current.Answering the phone normally for a fraction of a second stops the rings,but does not let enough current flow to start the billing. If you answernormally for even one full second, however, billing will start.
