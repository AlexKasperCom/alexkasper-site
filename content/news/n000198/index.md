'The Magician' surveys how premium pay-TV services (HBO, Showtime, Cinemax, and similar) distribute programming via geosynchronous satellite to cable headends and to over-the-air subscription broadcasters, and contrasts building illegal decoder boxes for cable or MDS signals against three technically 'legal' ways of receiving programming for free: home satellite dish reception, over-the-air MDS downconversion, and multipoint distribution systems. Covers the basic engineering of home satellite dishes (dish size, low-noise amplifiers, signal-to-noise tradeoffs) and points to contemporary hobbyist publications like 'Microwave News' and 'Coop's Satellite Digest' for further technical detail.
