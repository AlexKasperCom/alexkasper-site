The first module of the internal CSEPS training workbook, the print course manual Kevin Mitnick and Alex Kasper developed for Defensive Thinking's corporate social-engineering awareness course. It introduced the fundamentals of social engineering: what it is, why it works, who social engineers typically target, and the research–develop-trust–exploit-trust cycle underlying an attack.

Cited in the Wikipedia article on CSEPS. The workbook was internal course material distributed to enrolled participants; this entry records its bibliographic details only and does not reproduce its content.
