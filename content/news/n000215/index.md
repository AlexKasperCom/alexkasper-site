The first issue of TAP's standalone classified ad sheet, mailed alongside main issues 57 and 58. Lays out the ad-rate policy (paid listings, optional anonymous TAP Box numbers) and prints an early round of reader buy/sell/wanted/trade listings, launching what became a recurring companion insert to the main newsletter.
