Announces a new physical office in New York where volunteers and readers can help produce the newsletter, ask questions, or get back issues in person — with a strict no-illegal-materials policy for visitors. Publishes an 'Advanced Blue Box' circuit built around a 555 timer for better frequency stability than the prior 8038-chip design, corrects component values from the earlier Snoop Light circuit, and lists mail-order electronics suppliers for readers who can't find parts locally.
