Tom Edison warns that TAP is on the verge of folding due to unsustainable printing, postage, and rent costs, having just published six issues in one month to catch up on a backlog. Announces new fees for the reader information-exchange service and the end of free subscriptions (except for prisoners), pleading urgently for readers to recruit friends as new subscribers to keep the publication alive.
