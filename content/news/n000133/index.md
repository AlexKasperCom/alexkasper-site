A reader letter cites a Ramparts report that the NSA monitors and records all trans-Atlantic and trans-Pacific calls, and TAP asks Bell employees for details. Continues digging into ITT's business empire, publishes corrections and deeper technical notes on the Red Box (tip/ring grounding, IC oscillator drift problems), warns readers about a NYC subway slug scheme people are being caught for, and prints a country-code list for direct-dialing international calls.
