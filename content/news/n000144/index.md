Publishes the formula for the 1974 credit card billing code and solicits reader research on automatic blue boxes, lock picking, and burglar alarm bypasses. A reader details how to turn back a Con Ed electric meter without leaving telltale smudges, with a legal warning about New York's meter-tampering statute. The issue also documents direct evidence that Bell security has been monitoring TAP's subscriber mail — including a case where a Bell employee was found carrying an addressed copy of the newsletter — and calls it an illegal national surveillance operation covering roughly 600 people.
