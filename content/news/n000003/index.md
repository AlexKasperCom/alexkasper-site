*Cyberpunk* follows three strands of early hacker history, including the Southern California community associated with Kevin Mitnick. The book describes Lewis De Payne as a prominent participant in the Los Angeles phone-phreaking scene and discusses the HOBO-UFO conference line as a gathering place for phreakers and computer enthusiasts.

The book provides broader historical context for the transition from telephone-system exploration to computer-network intrusion and for the relationships among De Payne, Mitnick, Susan Headley, Steven Rhoades, and others.
