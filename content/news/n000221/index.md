Editor Tom Edison's candid, exasperated appeal to readers about TAP's finances — rising printing and postage costs, a new computerized mailing system that wasn't cheap, and lagging sales of TAP merchandise — capped with a threat to dock issues from subscribers who don't buy something. The usual classified listings and subscription price list round out the sheet.
