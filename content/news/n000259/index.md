This February 2003 snapshot of the Red Swoosh, Inc. website lists the company's address as "1100 Glendon Ave., Ste. 1700, Los Angeles, CA 90024," the same building and suite as Nexspace, and invites visitors to "get swooshed" by emailing "Red Swoosh founder Travis Kalanick" directly. Red Swoosh was a peer-to-peer content delivery startup Kalanick founded in 2001, years before he co-founded Uber; Akamai Technologies later acquired it in 2007.
