The 'Ammo' column compiles New York Times reporting on AT&T rate hikes, FCC/FTC regulatory failures, and a dispute over the Pentagon's phone bill. 'Don't Get Busted!' lays out phreak operational security rules — assume every call may be tapped, never trust too many people with your identity, and ask for a lawyer immediately if approached by the phone company. Includes a mechanical (rotary-dial-based) Red Box variant, improvements to the prior circuit, and a 'Line Relay' project for triggering devices when the phone rings.
