A special issue dedicated to outgoing co-editor 'Jim Phelps,' compiled entirely from material he wrote, commented on, or typed before departing after a year and a half at TAP; the editor's dedication thanks him for his labor and notes all names have been changed 'to protect the guilty.' Content Phelps left behind includes UK telephone-exchange fraud tips for exchanges using CAMA/LAMA operator identification, a reader account of a corporate credit card successfully validated by MCI's voice-response system, and a request for a debugged schematic for a radar jammer after a prior issue's radar-detector article proved popular. Also promotes the upcoming Technological Hobbyist Conference (THC-79).
