Continues a series by Paul Montgomery walking through how to dial into and navigate Bell System COSMOS terminals, internal databases used by phone company staff to look up wire-center and trunk assignment information for a given telephone number, including the command syntax for common lookups. A companion piece describes a toll-fraud method exploiting a firmware flaw in certain business 'Dimension' PBX systems that allowed callers to bypass an authorization code and reach an outside line, alongside a description of the long-standing 'lineman's trick' of tapping into above-ground or buried telephone drop wires with a test handset.
