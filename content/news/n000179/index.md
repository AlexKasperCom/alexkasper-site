Announces that TAP has formally renamed itself from the Technical Assistance Program to the Technological Assistance Program and moved to a new mail-drop address, marking the end of the mailing-label crisis chronicled in recent issues; publication now resumes a reliable triannual (Jan/May/Sept) schedule. Recaps the April 1979 THC-79 convention, which drew a large crowd for seminars on MF-tone signaling and even nuclear power, plus ESS training-film screenings. A reprinted item (poorly OCR'd) describes little-known special dialing procedures reserved for government, military, and post office officials on the public phone network, sourced from a leaked internal operating handbook.
