A special issue devoted entirely to defeating pay phone coin mechanisms: diagrams of old vs. new phone models, the 'spitting on pennies' and bobby-pin ground tricks, cardboard-strip slug methods, and ways to fake credit-card and collect-call billing to strangers. Abbie Hoffman contributes a column on 'pay phone justice,' framing coin fraud as a small act of resistance against AT&T.
