This Business Wire release announces the winners of the 1995 3DO Awards, which recognized games released for the 3DO platform between December 1994 and December 1995.

The awards covered categories including game of the year, action, adventure, simulation, strategy, and multiplayer gaming. *Return Fire*, published by Prolific Publishing, received the Two-Player Game of the Year award. The release also lists titles that reached the platform's gold and platinum manufacturing thresholds.
