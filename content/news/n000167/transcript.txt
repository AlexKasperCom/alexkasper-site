Liberal use of COSMOS integrated circuit techniques precludes 
even the need for an off/on switch, since the standby power on these 
is for all practical purposes, 0. ‘These are usually built in layers, 
and it results in a very vloume efficient device (sec illustration 
of hand-held unit). ‘Three layers consist of front panel with keyboard, 
memory and logic board, and tone oscillator/amplifier. It is my | 
feeling that the construction of an advanced unit of this type is 
best managed by individuals with a fair amount of electronic experience.

combinations.
