WorldCat's catalog record for *Eyeball Eddie* (OCLC 51101620) describes it as a DVD video, "1 videodisc (approximately 29 min.): sound, color; 4 3/4 in.," published by Pupil Productions, ©2001 (production dated 2000). It credits Elizabeth Allen Rosenbaum as director and producer, Alexis Kasperavicius as producer, Nick Pustay as screenwriter, and Matthew Jensen as cinematographer, alongside Pupil Productions and the University of Southern California School of Cinema-Television. Cast notes list Martin Starr, Michael Rosenbaum, Melissa Hunter, and M. Emmet Walsh. Credits also name Brett Hedlund and Tripp Reed as editors and Steve Weisberg as composer.

Its notes read "At head of title: USC School of Cinema-Television" and quote the end credits directly: "This film was produced in partial fulfillment for the requirement of the MFA degree at the University of Southern California, School of Cinema-Television." A separate note adds: "Originally produced in 2000; copyright held by Pupil Productions." WorldCat classifies the film under genres including short film, coming-of-age film, and dark comedy film.
