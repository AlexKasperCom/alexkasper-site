Return Fire is a single-player and two-player vehicular action strategy game. It is the spiritual successor to Fire Power.

The game is built around a military capture-the-flag contest between opposing bases. The player commands a small motor pool and must locate the enemy flag, take it from the opposing bunker, and return it to the player’s own base. The battlefield is filled with roads, bridges, water, hills, buildings, gun emplacements, mines, and enemy vehicles, making each mission a combination of scouting, attack, defense, and route planning.
