An introductory guide to unlicensed 'pirate' radio broadcasting, dividing the hobby into categories: low-power FM/AM band pirating, wireless-microphone-based broadcasting (limited to about a quarter-mile range), and carrier-current transmission fed directly into power lines or AC outlets to ride along the electrical grid. Discusses where to source suitable transmitter hardware (surplus and college radio supply stores), practical antenna choices, and typical broadcast patterns pirates use to avoid detection, alongside a blunt warning that the FCC can shut a station down and impose steep fines, though the piece notes many operators broadcast for years without incident.
