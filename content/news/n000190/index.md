A feature by 'Dr. Atomic' explaining, at a chemistry-class level, why cocaine hydrochloride (the salt form typically sold on the street) is unsuited to smoking and how converting it to freebase form lowers its vaporization temperature, describing the general process of separating the freebase using a solvent-extraction technique with household and paraphernalia-shop supplies. Framed as documenting a practice already popular in entertainment circles at the time, with commentary on the intensity and short duration of the resulting high and its compulsive redosing pattern.
