This company backgrounder, published on Nexspace's own site, describes an 18,000-square-foot "fractional office" facility on the 17th floor of the Westwood Center in Westwood Village, Los Angeles, opened in November 2000 and built to house up to 39 partner companies and roughly 200 staffers, combining private offices, shared "Development Rooms," a technology infrastructure (T-3 connectivity, private VLANs, an on-site server farm), and business support services.

It credits David Agger, "Co-founder and CEO," with originating the concept, and Alex Kasper, described as "an experienced data and telecommunications engineer," as the co-founder he recruited as Chief Technology Officer; the two "managed the development and financing of the company" together. Kasper is quoted describing the network infrastructure: "The pre-wired, plug-and-play nature of the Nexspace network allows our client companies to bypass the technology bottlenecks that plague many businesses."
