A reader Q&A column answers technical questions on tuning red box volume and warns that 'blue box detectors' are now being deployed. Publishes a fresh batch of credit card numbers and an expanded list of overseas country dialing codes. A reader describes Bell's internal operator-training films and secrecy indoctrination ('official communications secrecy act'), painting a picture of the company's internal culture, alongside a submitted limerick and continued mentions of the Captain Crunch defense fund.
