Wikipedia describes *Freedom Downtime* as a 2001 documentary directed by Emmanuel Goldstein and produced by 2600 Films, made in response to the 2000 feature film *Takedown* and examining Kevin Mitnick's prosecution, the Free Kevin movement, and public perceptions of hacker culture.

It names the film's interview subjects as Phiber Optik (Mark Abene), Bernie S (Ed Cummings), Lewis De Payne, Alex Kasper, Kevin Mitnick, and Emmanuel Goldstein, alongside *Cyberpunk* co-authors Katie Hafner and John Markoff, and Mitnick's grandmother Reba Vartanian. The film premiered at H2K (the 2000 Hackers on Planet Earth conference), had a limited theatrical/festival run, and was first distributed on VHS through 2600 before a two-disc DVD edition in June 2004 added roughly three hours of additional footage, including a January 2003 Mitnick interview.
