Editor Tom Edison opens with a New Year recap describing 1979 as financially rough for TAP, largely due to new computer/printer equipment costs and a steep drop in renewing subscribers, and asks readers to renew promptly rather than raise rates. The featured article, 'Better Living Through Chemistry' by Oz Y. Mandias and 'Agent MDA,' is a research guide for underground chemists pointing to Chemical Abstracts citations, U.S. and foreign patent numbers, and specialty journals as sources for drug-synthesis literature, and discusses the strategy of making 'legal analogue' compounds not yet named in controlled-substance schedules.
