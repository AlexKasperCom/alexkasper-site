Reviews Jacques Bergier's Secret Armies, a history of industrial espionage and exotic weapons research, at length. Reader mail covers free mail-sending using recycled business reply envelopes, offers of PCB fabrication for TAP projects, alternative-energy work (electric car performance, CB repeater design, diesel heat pumps), and a request for information on infrasonic and ultrasonic effects on the human body.
