'The Sagician' follows up on a prior issue's discussion of 1633 Hz signaling tones (used for toll-free loop-arounds, mass conferencing, and Autovon access) with a step-by-step hardware guide for adding the missing fourth column of DTMF keys to a standard Bell touch-tone phone. The mod requires only a miniature SPDT toggle switch and hookup wire, wired into the touch-tone pad's toroid transformers after opening the handset housing, letting the phone generate tones that consumer keypads normally omit.
