*Ghost in the Wires*, Kevin Mitnick's bestselling memoir co-written with William L. Simon, recounts his hacking career, years as a fugitive, arrest, and imprisonment in his own words, giving his first-person counter-narrative to the Shimomura/Markoff account in *Takedown*, from his early social-engineering exploits through the FBI manhunt and his eventual 1995 capture in Raleigh, North Carolina.

The cited passages (pp. 97–99, 233–243) describe Mitnick's long friendship and collaboration with Alex Kasper. They cover episodes from their early involvement with telephone and computer systems as well as their later professional relationship, including the creation of Defensive Thinking and their work educating organizations about social engineering and information security.
