In this memoir, Kevin Mitnick recounts his early fascination with telephone systems and computers, his years as a fugitive, his arrest and imprisonment, and his later work as a cybersecurity consultant and author.

The cited passages describe Mitnick's long friendship and collaboration with Alex Kasper. They cover episodes from their early involvement with telephone and computer systems as well as their later professional relationship, including the creation of Defensive Thinking and their work educating organizations about social engineering and information security.
