Reports the phone company's slow rollout of a new computerized credit card system and publishes the current code formula. Floats a reader project idea for a 'Portable Pay Phone' device that would electrically simulate a real coin phone without needing actual coins, and expresses doubt about the previously reported Telident call-tracing device after its manufacturer seemingly vanished. Includes a nationwide list of telephone 'loop' test-number pairs used to connect two callers directly.
