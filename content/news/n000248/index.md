This Wayback Machine capture of Barkley Court Reporters' company homepage describes the firm as serving "law firms, corporate counsel and the entertainment industry for more than 40 years," one of the largest privately held court reporting companies and the first government-certified green court reporting service in the U.S., with offices across California, New York, Illinois, and Nevada as well as Paris, Hong Kong, and Dubai.

It lists services including court reporting, videography, event transcription, online transcript/exhibit access, mobile videoconferencing, and interpreter services, and names the Golden Globes, the Emmy Awards, and the Television Critics Association among its clients.
