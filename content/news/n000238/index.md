This two-sided promotional postcard advertised Eyeball Eddie's screening at the Slamdance Film Festival in Park City, Utah, in January 2001. It credits writer Nick Pustay, director Elizabeth Allen, producers Alexis Kasperavicius and Elizabeth Allen, and cast members Martin Starr, Michael Rosenbaum, and M. Emmet Walsh.
