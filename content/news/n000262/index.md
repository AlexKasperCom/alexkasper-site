Naturesign's homepage positions the product as "the fastest way to generate regulatory documents for the flavor & fragrance industry," addressing that "many buyers won't even review samples until they have the full regulatory set." It lists more than 50 supported document types, including allergen statements, certificates of analysis, certificates of origin, and aflatoxin/mycotoxin/glycidol-free statements, and describes a three-step process: a one-time setup building custom digital templates from a client's existing paperwork, entering product/lot/customer details, and receiving completed, signed documents in about two minutes.

The page includes a customer testimonial from John Campoverde, Customer Service Manager at MCI Miritz Citrus, and states that Naturesign™ is a trademark of Rosewood Key, LLC, headquartered at 1900 N Bayshore Dr, Ste 1A-317, Miami, FL 33132.
