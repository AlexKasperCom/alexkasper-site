Jim Phelps devotes a column to Libertarian Party politics, endorsing 1976 presidential candidate Roger MacBride and framing government taxation as 'the biggest monopoly of all.' Reports that John 'Captain Crunch' Draper was sentenced to four months at a federal prison after FBI interrogation about phone and computer systems, with his employer intervening to reduce the sentence, and shares (likely disconnected) contact numbers for support. A satirical mock 'citizen's arrest' legal document parodies sovereign-citizen legal theory of the era.
