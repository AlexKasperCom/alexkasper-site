‘on the wall and it's either run ads or fold] Subscriptions are not coming in as fast 
as they must 
if we are to stay in business. New ways to raise revenues have got to 
‘be found and taking in ads looks like one of the best ways at this time. 
‘The plan at this time is to have one side 
of the 8 1/2

Blue Box for sale, good working condition, $100, write to Box A c/o TAP, 
‘This extra sheet can also be used to announce late breaking news items which cannot 
get printed in TAP because of publishing deadlines such as the new phone credit card 
code in January. I will have access to an offset press which means that 
I can print 
this sheet myself the day before I mail out the issues. This means that there will be no 
more omitting good items because of lack of space in an issue. They can always be 
included on the extra sheet.
