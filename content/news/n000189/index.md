Oz Y. Mandias's recurring column reports on DEA task forces targeting clandestine drug labs in major cities, warns readers that chemical supply companies have turned informant on customers, and reviews several counterculture books including 'Psychedelic Chemistry,' 'The Poor Man's Armourer,' and 'The Falcon and the Snowman.' Separately, 'The Wizard' previews an in-progress hobbyist project: a microprocessor-controlled 'brown box' (a combination phone-phreaking device merging several tone-box functions into one unit built around a National Semiconductor NSC800 chip), described as still in the design stage.
