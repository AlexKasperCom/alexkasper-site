The inaugural issue of the Youth International Party Line, launched by Al Bell and Abbie Hoffman as a newsletter fusing phone phreaking with New Left politics. Covers the 1971 credit card billing code, a simple hardware trick for wiring a second extension phone, and a homemade conference-call switch. Reprints Congressional testimony from Sen. Edward V. Long and CWA president Joseph Beirne on phone company wiretapping practices, alongside a War Tax Resistance appeal urging readers to withhold the federal excise tax funding the Vietnam War. Closes with reader letters and a teaser for an interview with Abbie Hoffman in the next issue.
