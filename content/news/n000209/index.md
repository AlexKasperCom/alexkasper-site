Promotes 'Brave New Con,' an upcoming science-fiction fan convention near Washington, D.C. where Cheshire Catalyst plans to run a vintage computer-game exhibit with Apple II hardware, and offers to bring bulk back-issue sets to conventions for readers who request them. A short 'Exchange Scanning' item notes that most Bell System telephone exchanges of the era had internal test lines and other undocumented numbers within certain blocks, a long-obsolete quirk of the pre-divestiture analog network that hobbyists sometimes explored out of curiosity, described here only in general historical terms.
