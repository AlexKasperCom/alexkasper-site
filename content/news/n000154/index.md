Mr. Phelps argues the Social Security Number has become a de facto universal identifier that enables cradle-to-grave government surveillance, and advocates a system of multiple, purpose-specific ID numbers instead. Tom Edison's 'Open Sesame!' revisits the 'Fortress' payphone coin-box access methods covered in earlier issues, critiquing the tools required by previous approaches and proposing a more practical technique.
