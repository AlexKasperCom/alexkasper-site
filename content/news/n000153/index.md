Columnist 'Mr. Phelps' pairs a tongue-in-cheek pitch for radical life-extension research with a constitutional argument for tax resistance, pointing readers to advocate Rene Baxter's materials. Tom Edison's 'Odds & Ends' column reports a New England crackdown on illegal phone extensions and answering machines and passes along countermeasures, plus a call for readers to report their local off-hook line current readings for a future reference chart.
