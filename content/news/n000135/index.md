Co-founder Al Bell writes an extended origin-story editorial framing YIPL as a deliberate provocation against the phone company as a stand-in for the military-industrial complex, previewing a coming legal showdown. Explains the Black Box for dodging message-unit charges, a whistled 2600Hz trick to flood Ma Bell's new blue-box detectors with false alarms, and launches a reader-info-trading department called 'Destructory Assistance.' Also covers commercial blue-box detector products on the market and a 'T network' circuit project.
