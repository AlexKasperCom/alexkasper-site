**Kasper Exchange** is a wholesale trade broker for spices, essential oils, flavoring compounds, and related products, founded by brothers Nick and Alex Kasper in 2010.{{< fn "n000260" >}} Per the company's own account, "Nick's deep understanding of the flavor and fragrance industry combined with Alex's knowledge of best-in-class technology, creates an industry-specialized platform that reveals trends and market insights to help our customers and partners succeed."{{< fn "n000260" >}}

Kasper Exchange is a Miami, Florida limited liability company, classified under NAICS codes 425120 (Wholesale Trade Agents and Brokers) and 425110 (Business to Business Electronic Markets). Its agents work on behalf of buyers or sellers in the trade of spices, essential oils, and flavoring compounds, without the company itself buying, selling, warehousing, or taking title to the goods; it does not sell to consumers, and its minimum order is USD 5,000.{{< fn "n000260" >}}
