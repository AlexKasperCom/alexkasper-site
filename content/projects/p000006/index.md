**Nexspace** was a technology-focused flexible office company founded in 1999 by David Agger and Alex Kasper.{{< fn "n000251" >}} Its first facility, an 18,000-square-foot space on the 17th floor of the Westwood Center in Westwood Village, Los Angeles, opened in November 2000, offering "fractional" short-term office space, furnished private offices and shared "Development Rooms," and a leading-edge technology infrastructure to as many as 39 partner companies at a time.{{< fn "n000250" >}}

Agger served as co-founder and CEO; Kasper, described in the company's own materials as "an experienced data and telecommunications engineer," was recruited as co-founder and Chief Technology Officer, and the two together "managed the development and financing of the company."{{< fn "n000250" >}} Nexspace was headquartered at 1100 Glendon Ave., one block from Wilshire Boulevard in Westwood.{{< fn "n000251" >}}{{< fn "n000250" >}}

Kasper's own tenure as co-founder ran from 1999 to 2006.{{< fn "n000253" >}} Nexspace itself was taken over by Techspace in 2010.{{< fn "n000251" >}}

Kevin Mitnick himself ties Kasper to Nexspace in the acknowledgments of *The Art of Deception*: "Kasper, [of] Nexspace, is not only my best friend, but also a business partner and colleague. Together we hosted a popular Internet talk radio show known as 'The Darkside of the Internet' on KFI AM 640 in Los Angeles..."{{< fn "n000068" >}}
