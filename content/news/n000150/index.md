Columnist 'Tom Edison' covers New Jersey Bell's discontinuation of shared party lines, framing it as a rate-hike tactic thinly disguised as a customer favor, and walks through wiring a home extension phone (including hiding the splice) now that private lines are mandatory. A 'Fortress Fun' piece covers repurposing a discarded 'Fortress' payphone as an extension, and a telephone-locks feature bluntly tells readers not to bother trying to pick residential lock hardware.
