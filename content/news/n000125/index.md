Opens with a correction to the tone codes misprinted in issue 2, followed by a reader-submitted phase-shift oscillator schematic for building a basic tone-generating 'toy organ' blue box. Reports at length on the prosecution of North Carolina's underground paper the Carolina Plain Dealer, whose staff were criminally charged for publishing the credit card billing code, framing it as AT&T using the courts to intimidate the underground press. A detailed 'how not to get caught making fake credit card calls' guide follows, plus a note on a Unitarian church in Hollis withholding the federal phone excise tax (with the Rev. F. Allen Wells Jr. quoted) to protest the Vietnam War.
