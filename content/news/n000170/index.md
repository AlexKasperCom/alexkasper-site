TAP's 7th anniversary double issue opens with a step-by-step guide (by 'Alexander Mundy') to fabricating lock picks and tension wrenches from spring steel. A second feature, 'Electronics for the Dope Dealer,' walks drug dealers through countersurveillance — detecting phone taps, spotting body wires, and scanning police/federal radio frequencies — followed by pieces on cable TV descrambling and other schemes, reflecting the era's drift from pure phone phreaking toward broader anti-establishment 'countereconomics' content.
