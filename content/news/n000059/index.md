*DarkSide of the Internet* was a weekly technology-oriented radio program broadcast on KFI-AM 640 in Los Angeles and co-hosted by Alex Kasper and Kevin Mitnick.

The program addressed computers, hacking, internet culture, privacy, and information security for a general radio audience. Its archived KFI listing documents the program's place in the station's schedule and provides contemporary evidence of Kasper and Mitnick's on-air collaboration.
