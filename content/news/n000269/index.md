Wikipedia's filmography for Martin Starr lists his screen credits in chronological order. Everything before *Eyeball Eddie* (2001) is either a guest spot on a television series (*G vs. E*, *Normal, Ohio*, *Ed*, *Mysterious Ways*) or a supporting ensemble role (*Freaks and Geeks*, as Bill Haverchuck, one of several teenage leads rather than the sole lead). *Eyeball Eddie*, in which he plays the title character, is the first credit in which he is the lead.
