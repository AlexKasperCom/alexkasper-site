With only a single ad submitted in time for publication, the editor used the extra space to print a complete alphabetical subject index to TAP issues 24 through 50 — compiled and typed by a reader credited only as "Agent MDA" — extending the index to issues 1-23 that had appeared in issue 24. One classified ad, seeking broadcast, security, and telephone equipment schematics, rounds out the sheet.
