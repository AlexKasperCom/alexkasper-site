IMDb's filmography for Martin Starr lists his acting credits in reverse-chronological order. Everything before *Eyeball Eddie* (2001) is either a guest spot on a television series (*Good vs Evil*, *Normal, Ohio*), a supporting ensemble role on a TV series (*Freaks and Geeks*, as Bill Haverchuck, one of an ensemble cast), or a minor role in a feature film credited under his birth name "Martin Schienle": "Biff Atkins" in *Xtro 3: Watch the Skies* (1995), one of roughly fifteen similarly-billed military characters in an ensemble film headlined by Robert Culp, and "Allen in Coma" in *Hero* (1992), an uncredited-scale bit part at age ten. *Eyeball Eddie*, in which IMDb's credits order lists him first as the title character, is his first lead role.
