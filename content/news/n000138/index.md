A reader letter argues for renaming 'Ma Bell' to 'Pa Bell,' pointing out AT&T's all-male power structure, a reframing the issue adopts throughout. Abbie Hoffman contributes a column ('We Try Harder') on free speech and paying for calls; other items include Stanford researcher Donn Parker's early profile of computer criminals, a prisoner's letter describing abuse in a federal 'behavior modification' program, note of Nixon appointing Kansas City police chief Clarence Kelley as FBI Director, and a wire anecdote about Israeli operators fired for eavesdropping on Golda Meir's call with Yitzhak Rabin.
