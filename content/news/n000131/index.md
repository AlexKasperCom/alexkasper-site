Announces the 'World's First Phone Phreak Convention,' the Celebration of Change, held July 11-15, 1972 in Miami Beach with teach-ins on phone politics and technology alongside antiwar and women's rights events. Publishes complete build instructions and a how-it-works explainer for a black box letting any phone receive long-distance calls free, noting the article mirrors one Ramparts magazine had published and then suppressed under phone company pressure. Reader tips cover pay-phone cash-box exploits and long-distance refund scripts.
