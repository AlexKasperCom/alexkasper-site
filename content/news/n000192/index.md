Opens with a history of U.S. fireworks regulation, noting that federal rules effectively banned firecrackers above 50 grains of powder in 1967 and that many states outlawed consumer fireworks outright, then frames the article as a hobbyist alternative to buying on the black market. The piece devotes significant space to safety procedures (eye protection, keeping chemicals away from flame, working with small quantities, using only paper casings) before discussing, in general terms, the construction of paper-tube devices and coloring/finishing techniques, along with pointers to chemical suppliers. Includes a disclaimer that neither TAP nor the author takes responsibility for injury from the information.
