Opens with a 'Twas the Night Before Christmas' parody by Jim Phelps and Alexander Mundy about a parking-meter maintenance visit. Announces a growing informal directory connecting readers by specialized skill (programmers, technicians, locksmiths, engineers, chemists) for mutual networking, and a reader recounts winning an out-of-court settlement from the phone company over unauthorized tree-cutting near their property.
