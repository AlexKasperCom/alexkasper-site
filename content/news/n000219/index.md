A notably substantive issue for a classified sheet: it carries an appeal from the Abbie Hoffman Defense Fund urging readers to write letters supporting clemency in his pending sentencing, previews TAP's forthcoming 10th-anniversary double issue and a planned "Best of TAP" anthology, and reprints a trade-press journalist's first-hand account of attending the WATS-80 phone phreak convention. The usual reader classifieds fill out the remaining space.
