Prints a harsh reader letter accusing editor Tom Edison of filling TAP with complaints instead of information, followed by Edison's lengthy defense recounting the newsletter's brush with financial collapse and chronic lack of reader support. A separate feature describes the emerging 'Automatic Telephone Payments' service letting customers pay bills by phone, an early consumer-convenience technology rather than a phreaking topic.
