Responds to reader questions about Ron Rosenbaum's Esquire exposé on blue boxing, confirming its technical accuracy (crediting box-builder 'Al Gilbertson') while noting some phone numbers were altered to protect sources. Restates YIPL's goal of becoming a reader-supplied information pool and publishes a table of 1972 credit card billing codes by city. Includes tips on avoiding detection when boxing from pay phones and a gag using a YIPPIE! button pin to reset a rotary dial.
