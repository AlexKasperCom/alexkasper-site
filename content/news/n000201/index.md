Editor Tom A. Edison opens the new year with housekeeping: restating that free subscriptions are reserved for prisoners only, tightening payment and legible-address requirements after a wave of unprocessable orders, and announcing a subscription price increase. A 'Security Alert' column by Oz Y. Mandias warns readers that itemized long-distance toll records can be obtained by law enforcement without a warrant and used as evidence of association, and separately cautions underground chemists that hidden radio tracking devices have been found hidden in chemical shipments during past investigations, citing a real sting operation as a cautionary example rather than a how-to.
