This Video Games (Germany) review characterizes Return Fire as a deliberately simple yet highly compelling action–strategy game built around relentless destruction and a Capture the Flag objective.

The article praises its focus on gameplay over graphics, the clear tactical roles of its four vehicles, and the ironic contrast between large-scale violence and rousing classical music. Overall, Return Fire is presented as one of the most engaging 3DO titles, valued for its addictive design, escalating challenge, and strong two-player mode despite potential controversy over its violence.
