A journalistic recap of Telecom '83, the ITU's quadrennial international telecommunications trade show held in Geneva, describing exhibits ranging from satellite earth-station antennas and fiber-optic transmission demos to a shortwave radio listeners' club booth and a UN-affiliated exhibit on bringing modern telecom infrastructure to developing nations. Cheshire Catalyst frames the piece around his view that technological progress in communications is inherently apolitical and beneficial, closing with reflections on how satellite links still ultimately rely on the same basic radio principles as older Morse-code era communications.
