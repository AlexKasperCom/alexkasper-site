This South Wales Argus review describes Return Fire as a standout shoot-’em-up for the 3DO, praising its fast-paced action, multiple vehicle types, and satisfying level-based objectives centered on collecting the enemy flag.

The article highlights its strong audiovisual presentation, varied perspectives, and explosive effects, noting that the game is immediately enjoyable solo but gains longevity in two-player play. Overall, Return Fire is positioned as one of the best titles available for the 3DO and a must-own for console owners.
