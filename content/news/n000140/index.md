Publishes a list of New York Telephone test numbers for foreign country dial tones, once public but quietly dropped from official brochures. Debunks a widely circulated D.C. rumor about a phone number (560-9944) that supposedly clicked if your line was tapped — even NBC anchorman John Chancellor tried it — explaining that it was actually an ordinary long-distance test/sweep-tone circuit, not a bug detector. Also covers automatic number identification (ANI) questions from readers and a back-issue catalog update.
