Tom Edison reports TAP survived its financial crisis thanks to a handful of supportive readers but lost a large chunk of its subscriber base, forcing a rate increase. 'Better Living Thru Counter Control' discusses obtaining a driver's license under an alias as a privacy measure against state tracking. Reprints a New York Times piece (by E. J. Dionne Jr.) describing New York Telephone's new computerized system for instantly tracing blue-box calls.
