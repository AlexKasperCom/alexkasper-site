Every confidence game, phishing attack, fraudulent investment, manipulative sales pitch, and manufactured crisis begins the same way: someone convinces you that you don't have time to think.

Manufactured Urgency is not a catalog of scams. It's a guide to the mechanics behind them.

Through real-world stories spanning cybersecurity, confidence tricks, propaganda, marketing, politics, and everyday life, Alex Kasper introduces the GRIFT Cycle—a model that exposes the recurring stages used by bad-faith actors to influence decisions before their targets realize they're being manipulated.

Rather than teaching readers what to think, the book teaches them how manipulation works—revealing recurring patterns that appear whether the target is an individual, a corporation, or an entire society.

Whether you're defending an organization, investigating fraud, studying psychology, or simply trying to make better decisions, Manufactured Urgency offers a new lens for understanding deception in all its forms.
