Returns after an unannounced six-month hiatus caused by short-staffing, promising more regular publication and teasing a planned 1975 convention. Publishes reader-sourced improvements to the New Blue Box circuit (matched diode parts, a diode-free pushbutton alternative), clarifies that Medeco and Keso locks resist the picking techniques described elsewhere, and reports a reader ('Stainless Steel Rat') prank-calling Boston phone security after being threatened with prosecution for publishing the credit card code. Also warns of an early caller-ID-like device from Telident Inc. marketed to catch obscene callers, and expands prior coverage of free-electricity meter wiring across different meter models.
