An editorial by 'Tom Edison' recaps 1975 as a year of Bell 'arson, greed, corruption, and invasion of privacy,' citing a suspicious fire at a NY Bell central office and revelations that the company recorded over 30 million calls hunting for black-box users. Lists the full TAP staff masthead (Al Bell, Tom Edison, Mr. Phelps, and others) and runs a detailed technical breakdown of black-box circuit design variants. A letter from John 'Captain Crunch' Draper reappears after his absence, offering to meet phreaks in person near Mountain View, California.
