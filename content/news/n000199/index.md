A reader-contributed roundup of mail-order parts sources for blue-box hobbyists, covering inductive telephone couplers for playing tones into a handset without opening it, 4x4 matrix keypad options and their tradeoffs (size, thickness, cost), and low-current crystal oscillator modules, with specific supplier names, addresses, and prices current to late 1982. Also comments on an earlier issue's coverage of a 'green box' built by pseudonymous phreaks Nick Haflinger and Ted Vail, and reports the author's own assembled box's standby and active current draw.
