A detailed how-to column by 'Alexander Mundy' on lock picking, covering the tools needed (tension wrenches, tweezers, a set of 'followers' sized to match plug diameters), how to fabricate followers from brass or plastic stock, and which cylinder types are easiest for beginners versus harder security pins like Corbin and Russwin's mushroom/spool pins. Walks through disassembling a cylinder to practice on, then building up skill from simple 4-pin cylinders to more complex 5-, 6-, and 7-pin designs, with a tip to mount the cylinder in a vise while practicing.
