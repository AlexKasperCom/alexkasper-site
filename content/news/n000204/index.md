Cheshire Catalyst shares Bell System 'rate step' tables, obscure mileage brackets phone companies used to price long-distance calls, discovered in a Vermont directory, and provides a short BASIC program that calculates airline mileage between cities from their internal grid coordinates for curious readers to experiment with. A companion piece by Fred Steinbeck follows up on issue 63's touch-tone modification article, noting that Pacific Telephone-era phones already ship with chips supporting the extra fourth column of DTMF tones and walking through opening the handset to access the module.
