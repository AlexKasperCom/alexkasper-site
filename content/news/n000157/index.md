Corrects an error in the prior issue's Red Box schematic, then prints a long dispatch from an isolated phreak in rural northern Canada, whose phone service runs through the Canadian National Railroad system and connects into a military/NATO phone network with its own priority-override tones — plus notes on early satellite-dish experiments and phone systems that resist standard techniques. A second reader letter describes a rumored RCMP surveillance device and an automated daily 'condenser check' system that detects illegal phone extensions and dispatches investigators, asking the readership for ways to understand or evade it.
