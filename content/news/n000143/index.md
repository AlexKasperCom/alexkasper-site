A cover editorial skewers the Nixon administration's response to the 1973 energy crisis as consumption-driven theater rather than real conservation. The bulk of the issue is a detailed, illustrated guide to residential electric meters — how to read, slow, jump, or bypass them — plus instructions for picking a 'rollersmith' meter lock with a bent nail. A Rumor Dept. column and a fundraising appeal for Abbie Hoffman's legal defense (post-cocaine arrest) round out the issue, with TAP crediting Hoffman as a major early contributor and funder.
