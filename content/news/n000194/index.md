Paul Montgomery explains how to use GTE Telenet, a nationwide packet-switched data network with local dial-up access in hundreds of cities, to reach remote computer systems by address rather than a long-distance call, aimed at hobbyists wanting to 'roam' between systems on Telenet's network cheaply. The issue also reprints correspondence from CENTAPP (Central Texas Area Phone Phreaks), a regional phreak group based in Austin, announcing itself to the wider TAP readership via a letter to editor Tom Edison.
