Reprints a Citizens Committee for the Right to Keep and Bear Arms (CCRKBA) campaign urging readers to lobby the U.S. Postmaster General for a pro-gun-rights commemorative postage stamp, framed as a low-cost way to counter gun-control advocacy groups. The issue also brings back former co-editor Jim Phelps for a one-off comedic 'Mission: Impossible'-styled column, with the customary disclaimer that all names, places, and events are fictionalized and that TAP disavows any resemblance to real activities.
