Kasper Exchange's "About Us" page states: "Founded by brothers Nick and Alex in 2010, Kasper Exchange covers all aspects of the spice and essential oil trade. Nick's deep understanding of the flavor and fragrance industry combined with Alex's knowledge of best-in-class technology, creates an industry-specialized platform that reveals trends and market insights to help our customers and partners succeed."

It describes Kasper Exchange as a wholesale trade broker whose agents work for buyers or sellers in the commercial exchange of spices, essential oils, flavoring compounds, and related products, without itself buying, selling, warehousing, or taking title to goods, and with no consumer sales (a minimum order of USD 5,000). The page identifies the company as "a Miami, Florida Limited Liability Company... founded in 2010," classified under NAICS codes 425120 (Wholesale Trade Agents and Brokers) and 425110 (Business to Business Electronic Markets).

The company's homepage similarly describes Kasper Exchange as offering "a competitive advantage in the commercial trade of spices, essential oils and organic compounds for food, flavor and fragrance," covering trending and markets, test comparison, regulatory compliance, and shipping logistics.
