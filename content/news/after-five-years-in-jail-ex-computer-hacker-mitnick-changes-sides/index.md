This *Ventura County Star* article profiles Kevin Mitnick after his release from prison and his transition into legitimate computer-security work.

It discusses Mitnick's efforts to use his experience with hacking and social engineering to advise organizations on security risks. The article also describes his collaboration with Alex Kasper through Defensive Thinking, presenting the company as part of Mitnick's attempt to redirect his knowledge toward prevention and education.
