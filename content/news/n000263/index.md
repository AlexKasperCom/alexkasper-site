Naturesign's "About" page traces the product to "a conversation at a leading global flavor conference (IFEAT)" that "revealed a universal crisis facing producers worldwide": a single flavor sample can require up to 30 customized regulatory documents. It quotes Alex Kasper, "Founder and President": "A single flavor sample can now require up to 30 customized regulatory documents. It's not impossible, just tedious, expensive, and exactly the kind of work that shouldn't require so much attention. Naturesign automates it."

The page states that "since 2010, Alex Kasper has worked in the flavor & fragrance industry through Kasper Exchange, a specialized essential oil trading company he co-founded with his brother Nicholas," and credits his prior role as "former VP of Technology at Barkley Court Reporters, one of the largest court reporting firms in the U.S.," where "he had designed systems generating millions of legal documents annually for complex litigation," as the basis for Naturesign's approach. It states Naturesign launched in early 2024 and has worked with partners including MCI Miritz, described as "one of the world's largest citrus oil suppliers," and that the platform runs on Salesforce.
