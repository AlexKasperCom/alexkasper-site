Reprints a New York Times piece on a Long Island blue-box arrest, then Al Bell explains the tone-pair frequencies behind blue boxing and a cassette-tape method for recording them without special hardware. The issue's centerpiece is a running published debate ('Dear Abbie'/'Dear Russell') between columnist Russell Baker and Abbie Hoffman over whether sabotaging the phone company helps or hurts the counterculture, with Hoffman arguing AT&T's monopoly profits justify the rip-off ('The Dumbest Rip-Off'). Includes early credit-card back-issue order info and reader letters.
