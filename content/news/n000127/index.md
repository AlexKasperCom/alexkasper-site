Explains the redesigned 1972 credit card code (moving the check letter from the sixth to the fourth digit) and a separate 'Code Billing System' exploit with a sample billing number. Encourages readers to prank-call Southern Bell's chief of security, Gerald E. McDaniels, at his office and home numbers. Includes an early write-up of what would become the 'black box' (letting a home phone receive long-distance calls for free) and a call to boycott ITT over its war contracting and PR spending.
