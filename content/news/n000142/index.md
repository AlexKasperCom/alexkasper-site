Describes building the 'Answeroo,' a relay-based device (built on the prior issue's Line Relay) that silently answers an incoming call and lights a lamp instead of ringing the phone, useful for late-night calls in a shared household. Continues the reader-driven 'Destructory Assistance' information-trading department, and opens with a Spiro Agnew anagram joke reflecting the newsletter's anti-Nixon-administration humor.
