This pre-production fundraising packet for Eyeball Eddie was assembled by Pupil Productions to solicit donor and industry support. It includes endorsement letters from Gary Fleder (Mojo Films), Martin Cohen and Erica Frauman (DreamWorks Post-Production), and Erin Simon (Red Hour Productions), a donor list, a production timeline, Nick Pustay's story synopsis, and the complete screenplay (WGA-registered January 1, 1999).
