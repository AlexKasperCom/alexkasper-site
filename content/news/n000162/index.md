An extended Jim Phelps interview with an anonymous self-described prankster nicknamed 'Guy Grand' (after the Terry Southern novel character), who describes years of elaborate hoaxes targeting institutions — including a fabricated reservation billed to a Federal Reserve official that drew Senate scrutiny and made national news. Phelps presses him on pranks with unwitting bystanders, and the piece reads as countercultural mischief journalism rather than a technical article.
