Publishes a redesigned Red Box circuit and construction notes, and appeals to readers to recruit friends to boost circulation rather than raise the subscription price. Reader letters debug regional variations of the 'T network' (Southern New England Bell wiring differences), discuss ground-wire tricks for pay phones, and continue the Destructory Assistance information-trading department. One letter is from a reader demanding removal from the mailing list over YIPL's politics.
