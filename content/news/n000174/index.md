Reviews 'Exotic Weapons: An Access Book' by Michael Hoy, describing it as a directory of suppliers and publishers rather than a how-to manual, and uses the review as a springboard for an editorial disavowing violence as counterproductive to political change. A short item points readers toward mail-order sources for lock picks and locksmithing tools. A hardware note describes a commercial MF (multi-frequency) tone-generator module that hobbyists could wire into blue box designs. The issue closes by publicly criticizing publisher Loompanics for distributing a Holocaust-denial title alongside its survivalist catalog, calling the book antisemitic propaganda.
